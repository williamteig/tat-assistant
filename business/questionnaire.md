-one is urgent audits with Greg. This is in the case the student books an audition or an urgent meeting and they want to go over their audition with Greg. This is a 1:1 session and is typically 30 minutes booked directly over zoom for a fee of $199.

- Other is ongoing bundles with greg, either with month on month or annual memberships. With this you get a private channel with greg in the membership site where you can directly upload material to him for him to review, book calls with him or just ask him questions directly.
